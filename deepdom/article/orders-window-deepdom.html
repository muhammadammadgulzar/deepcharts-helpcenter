Orders Window

The Orders Window gives you a centralized view of all your active and pending orders directly within DeepDom. It allows you to monitor and manage your orders in real time without leaving the platform.

Intermediate How-to 🕒 2 min read ⬇ Download PDF

The Orders Window gives you a centralized view of all your active and pending orders directly within DeepDom. It allows you to monitor and manage your orders in real time without leaving the platform.

How to Open the Orders Window

Click on the Trading menu in DeepDom

Select Show Orders from the dropdown menu

The Orders Window will open, displaying your current orders
