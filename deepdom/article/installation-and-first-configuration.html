Installation and First Configuration

This guide walks you through downloading, installing, and setting up DeepDom for the first time. Follow each section in order and you'll be ready to trade in minutes.

Beginner How-to 🕒 5 min read ⬇ Download PDF

This guide walks you through downloading, installing, and setting up DeepDom for the first time. Follow each section in order and you'll be ready to trade in minutes.

Before You Begin

Make sure you have the following ready before starting:

An active subscription at www.deepcharts.com

Your registered email address and license key (found in your Deepcharts Dashboard)

A stable internet connection

Administrator access on your Windows PC

Part 1 – Downloading DeepDom

Step 1 – Log in to Your Personal Area Go to www.deepcharts.com and sign in with your registered email and password. Once inside, navigate to the downloads section.

Step 3 – Extract the ZIP File

Locate and download the file named DeepDominstaller.zip. Save it somewhere easy to find, like your Desktop or Downloads folder. Right-click on DeepDominstaller.zip and select "Extract All." This unpacks the files needed for installation. Do not run the installer directly from inside the ZIP — always extract first.

Part 2 – Installing DeepDom

Step 4 – Check Minimum System Requirements

Confirm your PC meets the minimum requirements for DeepDom before proceeding. Running it on an unsupported system may cause errors. You can find the requirements listed in your account area at www.deepcharts.com/helpcenter

Step 5 – Run the Installer

Open the extracted folder and double-click DeepdomSetup.exe. If Windows asks for permission, click Yes to allow it to run.

Step 6 – Complete the Installation

Follow the on-screen instructions:

Read and accept the Terms and Conditions

Click Install and wait for the process to finish

Restart your PC if prompted — this is required to complete the setup

Part 3 - First-Time Configuration

Step 7 – Open DeepDom

After installation, a shortcut will be created on your Desktop. Double-click it to launch DeepDom for the first time.

Step 8 – Enter Your License Details

On the activation screen, enter the following exactly as shown in your account:

Email Address — the email you registered with at www.deepcharts.com

License Key — the alphanumeric code found in your Personal Area

Conclusion

You have successfully installed and configured DeepDom. Here's a quick recap of what you completed:

Downloaded and extracted DeepDomSetup.zip from your Deepcharts Dashboard

Activated the platform using your email (Deepcharts Registered) and license key

Connected your Level 2 data feed and selected your markets

Opened your first Heatmap and started receiving live data

You're now ready to explore the platform. Check the other Knowledge Base articles for tips on getting the most out of DeepDom.

Still Having Trouble?

Our support team is available 24/7 and ready to help you with any issue you encounter. When you reach out, please include:

A screenshot of the issue.

A brief description of what you were trying to do.

Support Ticket (fastest response): visit www.deepcharts.com and create a support ticket directly on our website.

Email Support: you can also reach us at support@deepcharts.com. Please note that email responses may take a little longer than a support ticket.
