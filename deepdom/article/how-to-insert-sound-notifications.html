How to Insert Sound Notifications

This guide shows how to enable and configure sound notifications in DeepDom, and how to associate them with annotations and indicators so that you receive an audio alert whenever key price conditions are met.

Beginner How-to 🕒 3 min read ⬇ Download PDF

This guide shows how to enable and configure sound notifications in DeepDom, and how to associate them with annotations and indicators so that you receive an audio alert whenever key price conditions are met.

Step 1 – Enable Notifications

📷
Screenshot placeholder

ID: dd-en-how-to-insert-sound-notifications-01 · save as dd-en-how-to-insert-sound-notifications-01.png

From the main menu bar in DeepDom:

Open Options .

Click Alerts .

Ensure that items related to Alert sound, Message Pop‑ups , Webinars , and Trading sound notifications are enabled.

This activates the global notification system so that sound alerts configured later will be allowed to play.

Step 2 – Add Sound Notification Profiles

📷
Screenshot placeholder
How to Insert Sound Notifications — Step 2 – Add Sound Notification Profiles (DeepDOM)
ID: dd-en-how-to-insert-sound-notifications-02 · save as dd-en-how-to-insert-sound-notifications-02.png

You can create reusable sound profiles, each with its own name and audio file.

Open Options from the DeepDom menu bar.

Go to Settings and move to the Alerts column.

Click Add to create a new sound notification.

Assign a descriptive Name (for example, Resistance).

Click the file icon to upload a sound file.

DeepCharts provides a set of default alert sounds in a folder similar to Documents\Deepdom\Alert Sound. You can also use your own custom audio files.

Select the desired sound file.

Click Open to load it and use the green play button to preview the sound.

Click Save to confirm the new sound notification.

Repeat the process to add additional sounds, then click Save Settings to store all changes.

Restart DeepDom to ensure all newly added sound notifications are loaded correctly.

Step 3 – Associate Sounds with Annotations

Once notifications are defined, you can link them to drawing tools so a sound plays when price interacts with those annotations.

Open a chart and add the desired annotation (for example, a horizontal line at a resistance level).

Select the annotation and right‑click it.

Enable Sound Alerts .

Click Notification Settings .

Choose one of your saved sound notifications from the list (for example, Resistance).

Optionally, add a custom text message that will be shown together with the sound alert.

Step 4 – Associate Sounds with Indicators

You can also attach sound notifications directly to indicator signals.

Open the Indicator Parameters dialog (from the Indicators panel or by right‑clicking the indicator on the chart).

Locate the Sound Notification section.

Enable Sound Notifications .

Select a notification from the list of defined sounds.

After these steps, whenever the indicator triggers its alert condition, DeepDom will play the associated sound and, if configured, display the warning message.
