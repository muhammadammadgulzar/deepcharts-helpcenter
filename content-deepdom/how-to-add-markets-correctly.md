This guide explains how to associate each market with the correct data feed in DeepDom using the Manage Symbols window. Proper mapping ensures that every market you trade receives data from the intended connection.

## General Procedure

[SCREENSHOT: How to Add Markets Correctly — General Procedure (DeepDOM) | how-to-add-markets-correctly-01.png]

Before changing market associations, verify with your data provider that the required markets are included in your subscription.

Disconnect the data stream and all active connections (remove the check next to the connection name).

Close all open Workspaces.

Go to Options > Symbols Manage .

## If Markets Have Not Been Added Before

[SCREENSHOT: How to Add Markets Correctly — If Markets Have Not Been Added Before (DeepDOM) | how-to-add-markets-correctly-02.png]

In Symbols Manage , click the Subscribe button.

In the top menu of the window, select the new connection you want to use.

Use left‑click (or Ctrl + left‑click for multiple selection) to choose the desired markets.

Click Select to confirm.

Close the window to complete the procedure.

## If Markets Were Added with Another Connection

[SCREENSHOT: How to Add Markets Correctly — If Markets Were Added with Another Connection (DeepDOM) | how-to-add-markets-correctly-03.png]

In Symbols Manage , select the existing market entry.

Right‑click on the link symbol and choose the new connection from the dropdown menu.

Close the window to apply the changes.

After updating the mappings, reconnect your data feed and reopen your Workspaces. The markets will now be correctly linked to the chosen connection in DeepDom.
