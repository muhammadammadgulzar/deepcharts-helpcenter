You can tailor the interface language, time zone, and visual theme of DeepDom from the General settings. This guide shows where to find these options and which languages and themes are available.

## Access General Settings

[SCREENSHOT: How to Customize Language and Theme — Access General Settings (DeepDOM) | dd-en-how-to-customize-language-and-theme-01.png]

1. Open Options in the DeepDom menu bar.
2. Click Settings .

[SCREENSHOT: How to Customize Language and Theme — Access General Settings (DeepDOM) | dd-en-how-to-customize-language-and-theme-02.png]

3. In the left column, select the General section. Here you can configure:

Language

Time Zone

Theme (platform appearance)

## Change the Interface Language

From the Language dropdown, choose your preferred language. Available options typically include:

English

Italian

Spanish

German

Chinese

After selecting a new language, restart DeepDom if prompted so that all menus and messages are fully updated.

### Select a Platform Theme

To change the visual appearance of the platform, pick a theme from the Theme (platform appearance) list. The following themes are available on VolSys/DeepDom:

Dark

Dark Green

Dark Light Blue

Blue

Light

Purple
