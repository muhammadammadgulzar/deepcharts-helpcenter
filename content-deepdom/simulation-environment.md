DeepDom includes a built‑in simulation environment that lets you practice strategies with SIM accounts using real‑time data but without financial risk. This guide explains how to create SIM accounts and trade in simulation mode.

## Add a New SIM Account

[SCREENSHOT: Simulation Environment — Add a New SIM Account (DeepDOM) | dd-en-simulation-environment-01.png]

1. Open the Options menu and select Settings .
2. Go to the SIM Account section.

[SCREENSHOT: Simulation Environment — Add a New SIM Account (DeepDOM) | dd-en-simulation-environment-02.png]

In this section you can add or delete simulation accounts:

Click Add to create a new SIM account.

Enter a Name for the account.

Set the initial Balance .

Click Add again to confirm.

## Trade in Simulation Mode

[SCREENSHOT: Simulation Environment — Trade in Simulation Mode (DeepDOM) | dd-en-simulation-environment-03.png]

To operate in simulation mode:

Open a trading panel or DOM in DeepDom.

Select SIM as the broker.

Choose the desired SIM account from the account list.

You can now place orders and manage positions in real time, but all trades will be recorded only in the simulation environment, without affecting your live accounts.
